# Making oxygen on Mars

## Can astronauts make oxygen on Mars? MOXIE results and limits (status: demonstrated on Mars, at small scale)
MOXIE, carried by NASA's Perseverance rover, demonstrated that oxygen can be produced on Mars from the carbon-dioxide-rich
atmosphere. Across 16 runs it produced 122 grams of oxygen. Its best rate was about 12 grams of oxygen per hour, at 98% purity or
better. MOXIE was a technology demonstrator, not a full-scale life-support or rocket-fuel plant: a crew-scale oxygen plant is still
needed (status: under development / proposed).
